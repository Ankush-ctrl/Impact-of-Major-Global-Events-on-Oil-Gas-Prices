## Results and Analysis

Why i choose to do Analysis on the basis of BRENT CRUDE OIL ~ Roughly two-thirds of the world's crude oil contracts are priced against Brent, making it the most important marker for global oil trade.

For this project, I used historical commodity price data from the WORLD BANK and analyzed long-term trends around major market disruptions including:
• The 2008 Global Financial Crisis
• The 2020 COVID-19 Pandemic
• The 2022 Russia-Ukraine Conflict

Key insights from the analysis:
1) Commodity prices often spike during periods of global uncertainty then after some time seeing a slump nature in price.
2) Energy markets become more volatile during crises and supply disruptions.
3) Gold tends to perform stronger than silver from early 90s to present time, reinforcing its role as a safe-haven asset
4) Brent crude oil is seeing a bulish nature in price
